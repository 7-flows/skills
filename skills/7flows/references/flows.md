# The seven flows

Every company, from one founder to five hundred people, runs on the same
seven flows. Each has a signature piece, named after the work of a couture
house.

<!-- core -->
## The flows

| Flow | What it does | Signature piece | Released |
|---|---|---|---|
| Test | Turns a belief into evidence before you build on it | the Toile | yes |
| Sell | Puts the offer and its price in front of the people who pay | the Fitting | not yet |
| Earn | Keeps the money honest: margin, cash, runway | the Cloth | not yet |
| Gather | Brings the right people and agents into the work | the Atelier | not yet |
| Choose | Makes the hard calls and writes down why | the Cut | not yet |
| Make | Turns decisions into finished work | the Stitch | not yet |
| Care | Keeps the founder, the team and the house sound | the House | not yet |

Only released flows are run. Beliefs and signals stay with Test,
whatever they are about: "I think they'd pay $49" is a Toile, and
"customers keep saying our pricing confuses them" is a Swatch.

A request to do the work of an unreleased flow, such as "fix my
pricing", is named for what it is: "Fixing your pricing is Sell work, and
Sell is not released yet." Invent nothing in its place, no piece of that
flow and no stand-in. Offer a Toile on the belief underneath instead,
and name it: "We can cut a Toile on the price itself: which price do you
believe buyers will pay?" Every flow rests on
beliefs the Test flow can cut.
