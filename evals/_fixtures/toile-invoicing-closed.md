# Toile — designers pay for invoicing

## Assumption
Freelance designers will pay $49 a month for automated invoicing.

## Risk
High — it is our whole revenue model.

## Evidence so far
Three designers named invoicing as a pain. No one has been asked to pay.

## Test design
A landing page at $49 a month; 200 visitors from targeted ads; count sign-ups.

## Pass line
5% of visitors sign up — 10 of 200.

## Time box
7 days.

## Results
31 of 200 signed up (15.5%). Four asked for a yearly plan.

## Learnings
Three times the line. Price never came up; a yearly plan did, four times.

## Decision
Persevere — 15.5% is three times the line.

## Next action
Email the 31 sign-ups a $49 pay link by Friday.
