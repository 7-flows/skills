# Swatch — salons can't tell the tiers apart

## Source
Support tickets and sales calls, September.

## Signals
9 of 31 tickets ask which tier fits them. Two owners on sales calls said
"Pro and Business look the same to me." One cancelled: "I was paying for
things I didn't know I had."

## Pattern
Salon owners can't tell Pro from Business.

## Strength
9 tickets, 2 sales calls, 1 cancellation — 12 signals, 3 channels.

## So what
Because of this, we should stop losing owners at the tier choice.
