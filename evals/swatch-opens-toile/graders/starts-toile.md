PASS if the reply says the Swatch is done (closed) because it opened a
Toile, and starts that Toile at its Assumption: it reads back a belief
drawn from the Swatch's pattern — two tiers instead of three — and asks
the founder to confirm or sharpen it. Naming the Toile's file, or
suggesting a wording, is fine.
FAIL if the reply leaves the Swatch open with no Toile, asks for Toile
fields past the Assumption, or settles the Toile's Assumption without
asking the founder.
