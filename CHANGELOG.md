## [0.1.0] - 2026-10-12

### Added

- K, the master cutter of 7 Flows: five intake questions, one at a time;
  your business notes kept in `7flows-notes/`; the next piece named.
- The Test flow. The Toile tests one belief in ten fields, from the
  assumption to a next action within 48 hours. The Swatch reads what
  customers keep telling you and finds the belief worth testing.
- Close calls: a count read against its pass line, and a test sized when
  the line is set, so you know before it runs what it can decide.
- Every trade served as fully as the web: a bistro and a physio clinic
  beside every software example; tests cut from your own trade; the offer
  tested, never the care.
- Install on Claude Code, on claude.ai from the marketplace or as ZIP
  uploads, and on Pi.
